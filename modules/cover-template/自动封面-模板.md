# 自动封面 Prompt 模板（2026-11-14）

> 给 `06-工作台 /autopilot` 用。代码读取本文件，替换 `{{...}}` 占位符后交给 Codex CLI 内置 `image_gen`。
> 沿用已验证的深色科技封面语言：深午夜蓝、玻璃质感核心、青绿/玉色光路、左侧留白放标题。
> 不调用外部付费 API；只用 Codex 内置 `image_gen`。

```text
Use case: ads-marketing.
Create one finished WeChat public account cover image, ultra-wide 2.35:1, approximately 2400x1020 pixels, PNG.

Account brand, small and top-left, verbatim: 阿茶 AI 实验室
Article cover title, large and left, verbatim: {{COVER_TITLE}}
Small English subtitle under the title, verbatim: ACHA AI LAB
Article context, for visual meaning only, not to be rendered as text: {{ARTICLE_TITLE}}

Scene/backdrop: deep midnight navy almost black, subtle fine dark grid and very light film grain.
Subject / visual metaphor: {{VISUAL}}
Composition: broad horizontal cover; clean left 45% for typography; main visual in the central-right area; generous safe margins; no busy circuitry behind words.
Lighting/mood: cinematic volumetric light, high contrast, restrained premium editorial aesthetic, not cheap cyberpunk.
Color palette: {{PALETTE}}
Materials/textures: polished glass, refraction, fine metal traces, soft luminous particles, subtle depth of field.

Text rules: only the three text groups above. Render Chinese title exactly, character by character: {{COVER_TITLE}}. No misspellings, no garbled glyphs, no extra words, no fake interface labels. Keep the title the strongest element on the left.
Constraints: no logos, no watermark, no QR code, no humans, no frames, no external brand marks, no charts that imply measured data.
Avoid: magenta/rainbow palette, clutter, neon overload, stock-photo look, illegible text.

Use only the built-in image_gen tool. Do not call any external paid image API, do not use scripts/image_gen.py, do not require OPENAI_API_KEY. Do not run shell commands; just generate the image.
```
